# Second Ear: 90-second demo script

**Before you go on stage:** run `npm run dev`, open http://localhost:3000, click the sample once, press Listen once, and play "Original speed" once. Everything is then cached and loads instantly. Turn laptop volume up.

---

**[0:00] Opening.** Second Ear is on screen.

> "A transcript moves the problem from listening to reading. Second Ear re-delivers the lecture at a pace an international student can actually absorb, then checks they understood it."

**[0:10] The problem.**

> "42% of international students say accents are what hurts their listening most. 36% say speed. Picture a first-semester student in stats: strong in math, and losing the lecture anyway."

**[0:20] Hear.** Click **Try the sample lecture**, then **▶ Original speed** for about 5 seconds.

> "This is what they heard: a professor going fast on interaction terms, with numbers spoken inline. StepAudio transcribes it, so this is what you heard."

Stop it. Point at the right-hand panel.

> "step-3.7-flash re-delivers the same lecture in short, plain sentences, one idea per line. Nothing is summarized away."

**[0:35] Listen.** Make sure **0.75x** is selected and click **▶ Listen**. Let 2 or 3 lines play.

> "StepFun's voice reads it slowly, and the line you're on lights up. Missed one? Tap it."

Tap a line to replay it.

**[0:50] Understand.** Click **2 Understand**, tap **interaction term**, then **🔊 Say it**.

> "These are the words that made it hard, in simple English, with an example and the pronunciation."

**[1:05] Check.** Click **3 Check** and answer the first question right and one wrong, so the explanation shows.

> "Then it checks. Not 'did you read it', but 'did you get it'."

Finish and land on the score.

> "You understood 2 of 3, and here are the two terms to review."

**[1:20] Close.** Point at the footer.

> "Three StepFun models: audio in, reasoning, audio out. A whole lecture segment costs under a cent. Every international student at every English-medium university. Next: live lecture mode."
